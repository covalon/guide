Even the dark days of a post-Cataclysm world need a splash of color to brighten them up every now and then! Citizens of Covalon looking to adorn themselves with the latest fashion need look no further than The Weaver's Web.

The store is owned by the mysterious Qetello Fate-Weaver (nonbinary human witch), a fortune-teller who inherited it from the former regent of Covalon and has since expanded it into a supply station for all adventurers new to the city.

Newcomers can often be seen walking into the Weaver's Web with a smile and walking out with gear and potions; Qetello is as charitable as they are enigmatic!
