The town of Middlemist is a town hidden away from the eyes of mortals. The inhabitants are all Leshies that have either been abandoned, outlived, or escaped the druids that made them. This town was prosperous due to the natural bounty of the forest and the high-quality potions they produced. They traded with other villages through select merchants that would come and go.

When the Calamity came, the forest was engulfed in supernatural darkness, and Middlemist hasn't been seen since.
