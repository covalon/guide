Though the gods have gone silent, many citizens of Covalon cling to their faith in hopes that the divines will deliver them from the horrors of the outside world. Covalon's chapel, staffed by NPC holy folk and the [[The Servants of Terra|Servants of Terra]] guild, endeavors to provide religious services for all of the different faiths in Covalon, as well as a place of rest for those who have fought their last battle.
