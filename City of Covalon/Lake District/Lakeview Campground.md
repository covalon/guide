For Covalonians who can't afford property or prefer sleeping under the stars, [[The Servants of Terra|the Servants of Terra]] have cleared an area of the forest to be used as a public campground. The ranger station is staffed by forest warden Kouzo (male goloma cleric), and any citizen of Covalon can obtain a permit to stay at the campgrounds as long as they like if they promise to respect the forest and pay a small fee.
