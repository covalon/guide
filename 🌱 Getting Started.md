## Covalon
First of all, welcome to the game and the Covalon server!

You'll find almost everything you need to know about our server in [[📍 Covalon Player's Guide]].

You'll want to read the whole thing eventually, but at a bare minimum, you should read the first three chapters to learn: Player Expectations, Character Creation, and Covalon Gameplay.

> [!tip] The Quick Rundown
> ##### Covalon: The Setting
> - Covalon is the surviving settlement of a post-apocalyptic world. It is full of survivors from all over, working to restore the world ever since the Cataclysm in 2016.
> - The Gods went quiet during the Cataclysm, but touches of their power remain in their clerics and champions.
> ##### Covalon: The Server
> - As a player, you have one character (for now!) but will play at many GMs' tables. To minimize table variance, we try to adhere to Rules as Written (RAW).
> - New characters start at Level 1, eventually capping out at Level 15.
> - We use default character creation rules + alternative ability boosts.
> 	- We DO NOT use any other variant rules such as Automatic Bonus Progression or Free Archetype, nor do we use any Third Party options.
> 	- Common character options are available to pick by default; anything rarer must get staff approval, either pre-approved on the allowlist or via ticket.
> - Covalon has text RP for downtime, but all adventures (combat, exploration, etc.) are done through voice and require: a working mic and a computer that can run Foundry (free for players, runs through the browser).

## New to Foundry?
 > [!info] Helpful Resources
> - [Foundry Demo](https://foundryvtt.com/demo) lets you try Foundry for free in your browser, no purchase or download needed. Bonus, it's set up for PF2e!
> - [PF2e System Wiki: Making a PC](https://github.com/foundryvtt/pf2e/wiki/Making-a-PC) covers how to make your character sheet in Foundry PF2e, from start to finish, with images.
> ##### Prefer Videos?
> - [Youtube: Player's Guide to Foundry VTT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u9UCFwiPk54) is a comprehensive overview on the UI from the developers themselves.
> - [Youtube: How to Play PF2e in Foundry VTT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzobL67MJNM) was made for an older Foundry system, but a lot of the guidance for the PF2e-specific quirks of the Foundry system are still relevant.

## New to Pathfinder 2e?
> [!info] Helpful Resources
> - [Archive of Nethys: Player's Guide](https://2e.aonprd.com/PlayersGuide.aspx) covers all the basics of Pathfinder 2e.
> - [Classfinder 2e](https://www.classfinder2e.com/) has an overview of all the classes and what they do <small>(make sure Starfinder Classes is ticked <b>off</b>!)</small>
> - [Pathbuilder 2e](https://pathbuilder2e.com/) is an amazing tool for building character sheets.
> ##### Prefer Videos?
> - [Youtube: Learn Pathfinder 2e](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL5LfnOlAZRY4LpzHfXp3TbT1bNf4vgRKm) is a comprehensive two-video playlist by The Rules Lawyer, covering all of the basics and common pitfalls.

GMing is entirely optional, and we recommend playing a handful of adventures first before giving it a try. Whenever curiosity strikes, though, the [[📍 Covalon GM's Guide]] is always there to read.

If you ever want to take that step, you can [\#create-a-ticket](https://discord.com/channels/802423566196539412/889551411438825492) and let us know you'd like to become an Aspiring Dungeon Guide.
